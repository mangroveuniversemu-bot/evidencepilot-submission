# AGENTS.md

Guidance for AI coding assistants working in this repository.

## Architecture rule

Four layers, and they do not blend:

| Layer | Directory | May do | May never do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Agent | `agent/` | sequence tools, narrate results | compute a number, choose a verdict |
| Tools | `tools/` | read artifacts, compute, hash | apply policy |
| Policy | `policy/` | decide verdict, claim state, escalation | compute a physical quantity |
| Evidence | `evidence/` | assemble, sign, refuse overstatement | change a verdict |

If a change requires crossing one of those lines, the design is wrong.

## Non-negotiables

- `EvidenceClass` has no `PROOF` member. Do not add one.
- A `HUMAN_DECISION` escalation never carries a recommendation.
- A claim produces at most one escalation.
- Finite verification can refute a universal claim; it can never confirm one.
- Example data is generated by `scripts/build_examples.py` and is synthetic.
